Zombie guide

Meet the horde

Every night brings someone new to your fence. Introduced by Adam, live on air.

Uncle Harvey

The farmer

Forty years he guarded this harvest from crows and foxes, and now he's back in his old clothes for the very last crop. Shoo him off the field, finger!

The basic walker: slow, but they come in crowds.

Rocky

The miner

Rocky got trapped in a collapse deep underground. He dug his way out with his bare hands... and kept the bomb, just in case. Throw him far, far away... my window is right here!

Carries a bomb. Throw him away from the house or use it on the others.

Dusty Dan

The gunslinger

Dusty Dan lost his last duel a hundred years ago and still hasn't noticed. Same hat, same revolver, same bad attitude. Draw faster, finger.

Shoots at the house from where he stands. Keep him at a distance.

Zara

The sprinter

Zara never skipped leg day, and it shows. Hurdles, fences, your defences: she runs right through them. Catch her before she laps the house!

Never stops running. Reaches the house fast.

Nadia

The night nurse

Nadia, the night-shift nurse, still makes her rounds, jumping from bed to bed... only tonight, the patient is you. Time for your injection? No, thank you.

Jumps and flips through the air. Hard to catch.

Volt

The electrician

Volt fixed power lines for twenty years, until one line fixed him. Now he crackles with every step. Don't touch him with a wet finger, and keep him away from my fuse box!

Crackles with electricity.

Bloaty Bob

The bloater

Something toxic is brewing inside Bloaty Bob, and it's under a lot of pressure. He's a walking bomb now. Pop him far from the house. Very far.

Floats, ticks and blows up. Keep him in the air.

Rivet

The masked slasher

Nobody knows what's under Rivet's welding mask, and nobody wants to. Slow, silent, arms like fridges, built like a wall. Good luck, finger.

Cannot be thrown. A walking wall: use your tools.

Big Bolt

The iron brute

Someone wrapped Big Bolt in armour and lost the off switch. Bullets bounce, walls crumble, and he never runs. He doesn't need to. Throw him, but lift with your legs.

Too heavy to pick up. Only tools bring him down.

Player guide

Know your night

The house, the helmets and every way to play.

Barricade

Shields the house. On Moonlight Farm it takes house health from x1 up to x6. In The Concrete Block it shields the tower, in five steps up to x6.

Farm stars: free / 2500 / 4500 / 6000 · Block stars: free / 2500 / 4000 / 5000 / 6000

Special slot

Each slot holds one special. You can never have too many: from 1 slot up to 9. Moonlight Farm starts with 1, The Concrete Block with 2.

Stars: free / 1000 / 1100 / 1200 / 1300 / 1500 / 1700 / 1900 / 2200

Helmets

A zombie with a helmet can't be grabbed. Knock the helmet off first (a special, a shot or a fall), then deal with him. There are two looks, an army helmet and a hard hat, and both work the same.

Ways to play

Campaign

Two campaigns, Moonlight Farm (31 nights) and The Concrete Block (30 nights). Every night is a new wave of the dead, new zombies and new specials.

Open from the start (The Concrete Block too).

Easy / Normal / Hard

Normal gives +20% stars, Hard +50%. An untouched house gives another +50%.

Clear every night in normal mode to open hard mode.

Last Stand

No last night: level after level until the house falls. Beat your own record.

Clear night 17 of the campaign to open the last stand.

Overtime

16 extra football nights: balls, whistles, fan horns and a giant football.

Clear the campaign to open the overtime nights and win more specials.

Sandbox

Your playground: pick the zombies and the specials and try anything. It hides a few extra achievements.

Open from the start; each zombie joins it after you win its first night.

Duel

Split your specials: some to guard your own house, some to send tough zombies over to your rival.

Two players on the same local network. No server, nothing online.
