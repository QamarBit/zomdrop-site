Player guide

Know your night

The house, the helmets and every way to play.

Barricade

Shields the house. On Moonlight Farm it takes house health from x1 up to x6. In The Concrete Block it shields the tower, in five steps up to x6.

Special slot

Each slot holds one special. You can never have too many: from 1 slot up to 9. Moonlight Farm starts with 1, The Concrete Block with 2.

Helmets

A zombie with a helmet can't be grabbed. Knock the helmet off first (a special, a shot or a fall), then deal with him. There are two looks, an army helmet and a hard hat, and both work the same.

Ways to play

Campaign

Two campaigns, Moonlight Farm (31 nights) and The Concrete Block (30 nights). Every night is a new wave of the dead, new zombies and new specials.

Open from the start (The Concrete Block too).

Easy / Normal / Hard

Normal gives +20% stars, Hard +50%. An untouched house gives another +50%.

Clear every night in normal mode to open hard mode.

Last Stand

No last night: level after level until the house falls. Beat your own record.

Clear night 17 of the campaign to open the last stand.

Overtime

16 extra football nights: balls, whistles, fan horns and a giant football.

Clear the campaign to open the overtime nights and win more specials.

Sandbox

Your playground: pick the zombies and the specials and try anything. It hides a few extra achievements.

Open from the start; each zombie joins it after you win its first night.

Duel

Split your specials: some to guard your own house, some to send tough zombies over to your rival.

Two players on the same local network. No server, nothing online.
