Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short: ZomDrop does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Your progress is saved on your own device.

1. Who we are

ZomDrop is a mobile game made by Qamar Bit, an independent game studio based in Kuwait ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the ZomDrop game and to the website zomdrop.com.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data through the game.

  • No account or sign-up is required to play.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, or profiling.
  • No third-party SDKs. The game is built with the Unity engine.

3. Game progress

Your progress and settings are saved locally on your device. We have no access to them. If device backup is enabled in your phone's settings, your operating system (Android or iOS) may include the game's save data in the backup stored in your own Google or Apple account. That backup is managed by Google or Apple under your account, not by us.

4. Permissions the game uses

  • Network: used only for the optional two-player duel between two devices on the same Wi-Fi network. The devices connect to each other directly. Nothing is sent to our servers; we do not run any game servers.
  • Storage / Photos: used only when you choose to save a picture from the game to your device.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during play.

5. Purchases

The game currently has no in-app purchases. If purchases are added in the future, payments will be handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store, and this policy will be updated before that happens.

6. Children

Because the game does not collect personal data from anyone, it does not collect personal data from children.

7. This website

zomdrop.com is a simple static website. It uses no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking scripts. Like any website, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.

8. Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write in your message. We use it only to reply to you and do not share it.

Contact: support@zomdrop.com

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
